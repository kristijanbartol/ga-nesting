\subsection{Baselines}
\label{sec:baselines}

We compare against two baselines, both of which fix all seam positions at the
midpoints of their respective landmark quads ($\boldsymbol{\delta} = 0.5$)
and skip the Stage~2 global phase alignment.

\paragraph{B0: Pure greedy nesting.}
All phase offsets are set to zero ($\boldsymbol{\kappa} = \mathbf{0}$),
all patches are placed without rotation ($\boldsymbol{\rho} = \mathbf{0}$),
and patches are nested in descending order of area using the bottom-left
heuristic.
This baseline is representative of the classical nesting approach used in
industry, which optimises fabric efficiency with no texture awareness.

\paragraph{B1: Greedy seam-order nesting with greedy phase selection.}
Patches are placed in breadth-first order over the seam adjacency graph,
starting from the largest patch in each connected component.
This ordering ensures that when a patch is placed, most of its seam
neighbours have already been assigned a phase offset.
The phase offset $\kappa_i$ for each patch is then chosen greedily by
exhaustively evaluating all $K$ candidates and selecting the one that
minimises the total weighted mismatch with already-placed seam neighbours.
Grain orientations remain fixed at $\boldsymbol{\rho} = \mathbf{0}$.
This baseline captures the best texture alignment achievable by a greedy
strategy without global optimisation or seam-position search.
